## Description (paste into the page body)

### 🧋 Run your own bubble tea shop in a Bangkok soi!

Open the shutters of a little shophouse in a lively Thai street and serve **bubble tea** to everyone who passes by: students on their way to school, office workers late for a meeting, tourists, the auntie next door and delivery riders in a hurry.

**Make every drink by hand:** grab a cup, pour Thai tea, matcha, taro or red lime soda, scoop pearls, add ice, set the sweetness, seal the cup and serve. Fast and perfect drinks earn tips and combos.

**Features**
- 🧋 7 drinks, pearls, ice, 5 sweetness levels and a cup sealer. Watch the drink slosh as you move.
- 🏙️ A living soi: shophouses, tangled power lines, tuk-tuks, scooters and taxis, street food stalls and a spirit house.
- 🌦️ Day & night cycle, seasons and weather. Afternoon thunderstorms bring umbrellas, wet glowing streets and more customers sheltering from the rain (and they want hot drinks!).
- 📅 Daily events (Payday, Songkran, the night market, a reviewer visit…) and daily goals.
- ⬆️ Level up, unlock drinks, buy upgrades and restock ingredients at closing time.
- 💾 Auto-save every day, load any previous day.
- 🎓 A first-day tutorial, and the whole game in **English and Thai**.

**Controls (PC):** WASD walk · Mouse look · Left-click / E use · Right-click / Q throw a cup away · Esc pause · Tab skip the tutorial

## Uploading (you do this, logged in to your itch.io account)
1. itch.io ▸ Dashboard ▸ **Create new project**, then fill in the settings above.
2. **Uploads ▸ Upload files** ▸ `Builds/ThaiBobaShop_Windows_v1.0.0.zip` ▸ tick **Windows**.
3. Add the cover, screenshots and description, then **Save** (keep it as *Draft* until the preview looks right, then switch it to *Public*).

Or with **butler** (itch.io's upload tool, after `butler login`):
```
butler push "D:/Project-Start/A4/Builds/itch_windows" <your-itch-username>/thai-boba-shop:windows --userversion 1.0.0
```

## Updating later
After a new build, push the same channels again (butler only uploads what changed):
```
butler push "D:/Project-Start/A4/Builds/itch_windows" hoykhong/thai-boba-shop:windows --userversion 1.0.1
butler push "D:/Project-Start/A4/Builds/Android/ThaiBobaShop_v1.0.1.apk" hoykhong/thai-boba-shop:android --userversion 1.0.1
```
(butler.exe lives in %APPDATA%/itch/broth/butler/versions/15.21.0/)
